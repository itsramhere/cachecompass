# Candidate contribution and gate rationale

Phase 1 identifies **ElMem (R02)** as the nearest fully read direct research approach. The testable difference is the measured effect of joint, causal, incremental bucket admission and discretionary DB warmup under time-varying foreground DB allowance and donor pressure, compared with migration-before-cutover and isolated adaptive controls. See the [matrix](LITERATURE_MATRIX.md) for architectural distinctions and exact sections; [H1 and H4](HYPOTHESES.md) supply refutation conditions.

The study will proceed as **transparent reproduction-inspired characterization**, within the original scope. The verified unreviewed guide G01 describes essentially the broad joint mechanism; NetMigrate, ELICA and Holpaca also establish substantial coordinated-control prior art. The search therefore does not support a first-joint-controller claim. It is unnecessary to change topic to produce useful, honest findings about when these choices help or fail.

Candidate result: a reproducible local implementation and controlled comparison quantifying the latency/progress trade-off and failure boundaries under changing database/donor headroom. A positive result must survive strong migration-before-cutover, adaptive-budget migration-before-cutover, fixed schedules and both single-action ablations at matched finite completion requirements. A null result showing simple migration-before-cutover is adequate is equally within scope. Any distinct algorithmic claim must be reassessed after implementation and further literature review.

Backend-aware scaling, hot-key selection, demand-per-byte priority, feedback throttling, readiness-aware routing and warming before membership changes are not individually claimed as new. A read-count heuristic offers no universal safety guarantee; immutable single-router correctness says nothing about arbitrary writes or multiple routers. Independent Redis instances differ from native cluster resharding. Inspired local comparisons must not be advertised as reproductions of full switch, Memcached or HCI systems.

Gate: nearest verified approach identified (R02); at least one falsifiable comparison documented (H1/H4); overlapping joint guidance disclosed (G01); same-scope characterization chosen (DECISION-0004). AdaptCache remains inaccessible (ISSUE-0004), limiting exhaustive novelty assessment. This does not prevent testing the specified verified comparison. No measured superiority or publication readiness has been established.
